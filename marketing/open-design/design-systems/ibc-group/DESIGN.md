# ibc group

> Category: Professional Services
> Group brand for ibc group companies (including Etil). Black and white core, seven-colour ibc spectrum used sparingly, Ubuntu throughout.

Source: *ibc group – Brand Guideline 2026-04*. When in doubt, follow that guideline; this file summarises it for generation.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

The visual language of ibc reflects **connection, clarity and progress**. Black and white form the core of the brand; the **ibc spectrum** adds a vibrant, distinctive colour system that reflects the group's diversity, innovation and future-facing mindset. Layouts are calm, precise and uncluttered, with the spectrum used intentionally and sparingly.

Claim: **connecting performance.** (lowercase, with the full stop).

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Core
- **Night** (`#121212`): text, titles, dark sections.
- **Seasalt** (`#F8F9FA`): light backgrounds and content surfaces.
- White `#ffffff` and black form the visual core.

### The ibc spectrum — individual colours, unified identity
Each colour can represent a content cluster such as data, technology, process or people:
- **Violet** `#330099`
- **Deep Blue** `#000099`
- **True Blue** `#0066cc`
- **Shamrock Green** `#009966`
- **Vermillion Red** `#ff3333`
- **Pumpkin Orange** `#ff6600`
- **Bright Yellow** `#ffff33`

Apply them to illustrations and infographics, background accents, highlight elements and topic-based visual coding across business areas. Use them intentionally and sparingly, with high contrast, next to the black-and-white core. The full spectrum as a gradient (violet → blue → green → red → orange → yellow) is the brand's signature connector, as in the logo bar — use it as a thin bar or divider only.

## 3. Typography

- **Ubuntu** for everything; Arial as fallback.
- Titles in Ubuntu **Bold**; sublines and key messages in Ubuntu **Light**; body in Ubuntu **Regular**; Medium for highlights, subheadings and callouts.

## 4. Logo Rules

- Use only the approved versions in `assets/`. Keep the clear space, never recolour, crop, rotate, stretch or distort the logo, and never place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
- Do not overlap the spectrum with typography or logo elements.

Assets (in a studio project they are copied to `brand/ibc-group/`):
- **Dark backgrounds** (LinkedIn posts, title slides): `ibc-group-logo-white.png` — the official light lockup with the **colour spectrum bar** between "ibc group" and "connecting performance.", exactly as in the example posts. Always use this one on dark backgrounds.
- **Light backgrounds**: `ibc-group-logo.png` — the same lockup in black, also with the spectrum bar.
- `ibc-group-logo-alt.png` is the single-colour light version with a **white** bar. Do not use it for posts or slides: the examples always show the spectrum bar.
- Never draw, recreate, recolour or generate a logo or its bar in HTML or CSS; place the PNG as it is.

The authenticated design system may also contain `assets/private-library/` with the original brand guidelines, Office masters, logos, LinkedIn templates and image library. These are references; select only the files needed for a project. Creative Commons images require a fresh source/licence check and attribution before use. Realistic AI imagery in external posts needs a label directly on the image.

## 5. Tone of Voice

Precise, human and approachable; not loud, but strong.
- **Clear** — say what we mean, no jargon.
- **Human** — approachable, conversational and respectful.
- **Confident** — knowledgeable, never arrogant.
- **Precise** — every word serves a purpose.

Never invent customers, results, figures or capabilities: only state what the supplied Business Context or source material supports.

## LinkedIn post pattern (from the official templates)

Portrait 4:5 (1080×1350). **Before you design, open the example for the topic** (`brand/ibc-group/examples/linkedin-people.jpg`, `-data`, `-process`, `-technology`) and look at it; before you finish, compare your post with it side by side and fix every difference in logo, label, type and spacing.
- Full-bleed dark image in the colour of the topic, darker on the left so text reads.
- Top left (about 55px from the left, 120px from the top): a short thin white rule (about 95×2px), "Perspective on" in Ubuntu Light (about 36px), then the topic in capitals in Ubuntu Medium in its spectrum colour: DATA `#ff3333`, PEOPLE `#ff6600`, PROCESS `#009966`, TECHNOLOGY `#0066cc`.
- One short statement in white Ubuntu Bold, large (about 76px, line height 1.15), three to five lines, ending with a full stop.
- Bottom left (55px from the left and bottom): `brand/ibc-group/ibc-group-logo-white.png`, about 290px wide, with its **spectrum bar** — never the white-bar version.
- Bottom right (55px from the right and bottom): the AI label below, when the image is AI-generated. All service images in `brand/ibc-group/images/` are AI-generated, so a post with one of them carries the label.

## 6. Layout & Components

- White or Seasalt content surfaces; Night for title and key-message sections.
- Flat cards with a hairline border and a small radius (4–8px); no heavy shadows.
- A spectrum colour as a small marker (dot, tag, left border) to code a topic; never large fills of several spectrum colours at once.
- Footer line on documents: "Connecting performance. | © ibc group".
