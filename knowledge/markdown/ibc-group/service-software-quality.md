# Software quality: Secure, scalable, sustainable.

Quality Engineering Advisory from ibc group strengthens your software from the inside out. With proven methods, modern tools and experienced specialists, releases become more reliable, users more satisfied and your systems ready for future growth.

## Your Business Impact.

### Fewer production incidents

Stable releases reduce outages, hotfixes and firefighting in critical systems.

### Higher user satisfaction

Better quality means fewer defects, smoother journeys and more trust in your digital services.

### Faster, safer releases

Automated tests and quality gates allow shorter release cycles without losing control.

### Sustainable quality culture

Teams learn to think and work “quality-first” – beyond a single project or go-live.

## What you can expect from us:

### Software quality consulting

Analysis of your current quality processes and tool landscape, identification of weaknesses and design of tailored improvement measures.

### Automated tests & quality gates

Setup and integration of automated tests and quality gates into your CI/CD pipelines to secure every release.

### Test management with overview

Introduction or optimisation of test management with tools such as Jira/Xray or TestRail for transparent coverage and seamless processes in agile teams.

### Quality-first culture in the team

Workshops, coaching and best practices (e.g. Scrum, SAFe, Definition of Done) to anchor quality awareness in development and product teams.

### Experienced QA experts on demand

Flexible reinforcement with test engineers, QA leads and quality coaches – project-based, agile and method-safe.

## How we work:

Software quality is not a single test phase, but a continuous discipline across the lifecycle.

Engagements start with an honest look at how quality is organised today: processes, tools, roles and real pain points in delivery. Based on this, a pragmatic roadmap emerges – from quick wins in automation to structural improvements in test strategy, environments and responsibilities.

Collaboration happens directly with development, product and operations teams. The goal: quality practices that fit your way of working and can be maintained long after the project has ended.

### Assess & align

Review of quality processes, tools and roles, plus clarification of goals and constraints together with key stakeholders

### Design & implement

Definition of test strategies, selection and setup of tools, implementation of automated tests and quality gates

### Embed & improve

Coaching, metrics and continuous improvement loops so quality practices become part of everyday delivery

## Our Methods & Tools

### Approaches & practices

Test strategies, risk-based testing, shift-left quality, TDD/BDD, Definition of Done and quality gates in agile and hybrid environments.

### Test automation & CI/CD

Unit, API and UI test automation, regression suites and integration into CI/CD pipelines to secure frequent releases.

### Test management & reporting

Use of tools such as Jira/Xray, TestRail or similar solutions for planning, coverage tracking and clear reporting across teams.

### Quality metrics & feedback loops

Defect trends, coverage indicators, lead times and stability KPIs that make software quality visible and manageable.

FAQ

## Ask us anything

### When is it worth investing in dedicated software quality work?

As soon as defects, incidents or slow releases start to block business goals, a structured quality approach pays off quickly – especially for systems with many users or high risk.

### Do you only work with agile teams?

No. Quality engineering works in classic, agile and hybrid setups. Methods are adapted to your delivery model and governance.

### Can existing tools and frameworks be reused?

In most cases, yes. Existing tools and test assets are used as a starting point; gaps are closed and structures improved rather than replaced without reason.
