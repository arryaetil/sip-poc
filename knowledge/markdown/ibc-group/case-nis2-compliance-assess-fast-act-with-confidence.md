Context

## New regulatory requirements for cybersecurity and governance

With NIS2, the European Union increases cybersecurity and risk management requirements for many organizations. Affected entities must demonstrate appropriate security measures, manage risks systematically, and report security incidents in a structured way. The ibc NIS2 Readiness Check creates transparency on your current status and identifies the actions needed to reach compliance.

Challenge

## A structured NIS2 compliance assessment with a concrete roadmap

Many organizations are unsure whether NIS2 applies to them and what exactly needs to be implemented. Often, there is limited transparency over existing security measures and no structured assessment of cybersecurity maturity against NIS2 requirements. The consequence: unclear compliance can create regulatory risk and increase exposure to security threats.

Typical key issues:

- Unclear NIS2 applicability and scope of obligations

- No structured cybersecurity maturity assessment

- Incomplete security documentation

- Missing or inconsistent risk analysis

- No prioritized roadmap to close compliance gaps

What we do

We run a structured review of your security organization and controls and assess them against NIS2 requirements. The result is a clear, prioritized action plan you can execute and govern.

Typical steps:

- Review existing security measures and documentation

- Stakeholder interviews (e.g., IT, security, risk, compliance, management)

- Assessment against NIS2 requirements (governance, risk management, incident handling, reporting)

- Identification of compliance gaps and security weaknesses

- Cybersecurity maturity rating (baseline)

- Prioritized roadmap with concrete measures, sequencing, and quick wins

Typical deliverables:

- NIS2 gap analysis report

- Cybersecurity maturity assessment

- Prioritized measure backlog

- Implementation roadmap to reach NIS2 compliance
