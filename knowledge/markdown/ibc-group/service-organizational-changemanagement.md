# Change that people drive forward.

We help organisations turn change initiatives into everyday practice. With clear direction, honest communication and practical support, we align people, processes and structures so transformation really takes hold.

## Your Business Impact:

### Higher adoption of change

New processes, systems and behaviours are actually used – not quietly bypassed.

### Less resistance, more engagement

Involvement helps people understand the reasons for change.

### Faster realisation of benefits

Clear change roadmaps and focused interventions bring value forward in time.

### More resilient organisation

Teams build the mindset and skills to handle ongoing change with more confidence.

## What you can expect from us:

### Change strategy & case for change

Clarifies why change is needed, what success looks like and how it connects to your strategy.

### Stakeholder & impact analysis

Identifies who is affected, how strongly and what they need to move from awareness to commitment.

### Change roadmap & governance

Defines a pragmatic roadmap with clear roles, responsibilities and decision forums.

### Communication & storytelling

Makes change tangible and understandable through targeted messages, formats and channels.

### Leadership & sponsor enablement

Prepares leaders and sponsors to take a visible, credible role throughout the change.

### Training, enablement & adoption tracking

Provides training, on-the-job support and KPIs to measure and steer adoption over time.

## How we work:

Change is never just a slide deck or a campaign – it happens in conversations, routines and decisions.

We combine proven change frameworks with a clear understanding of your organisation’s reality. Together, we define what needs to change, who must be involved and how we make progress visible.

Throughout the programme, we work closely with your leaders, project teams and HR/communications functions. Our focus: honest dialogue, manageable steps and practical support that fits your culture.

### Assess & understand

We analyse the change context, stakeholders, impacts and readiness and define a clear case for change

### Design & mobilise

We co-create the change approach, roadmap and communication plan, and equip leaders and change agents

### Implement & embed

We support execution, enablement and feedback loops and adjust activities based on adoption data and reality

## Our Methods & Tools

### Frameworks & approaches

Change models like ADKAR, Kotter and Prosci, combined with agile and project-based delivery

### Analysis & diagnostics

Stakeholder mapping, impact assessments, change readiness checks and pulse surveys

### Communication & collaboration tools

Storylining, communication planning, content creation and collaboration via platforms such as M365, Teams and digital whiteboards

### Enablement & adoption tracking

Training design, change agent networks, adoption KPIs and feedback mechanisms to sustain change

## Ask us anything

### When do we need dedicated change management for a project?

Whenever a project significantly impacts roles, processes, behaviours or culture, structured change management dramatically increases the chances of success.

### Do you only support large transformation programmes?

No. We work on both large-scale initiatives and focused changes – from system rollouts to organisational adjustments.

### Can you work with our internal change or HR team?

Yes. We often act as a partner and accelerator for internal teams, bringing methods, capacity and an external perspective.
