# We turn fragmented transformation into connected performance.

By aligning data, technology, process and people, we make your projects coherent, measurable and sustainable.

## Who we are:

ibc group is a European consulting and technology group focused on complex transformation.

We help organisations navigate change by combining strategic clarity, technical expertise and a deep understanding of people and organisations.

With teams across Germany and the Netherlands, we work hands-on from the first idea to measurable implementation.

### Independent & European

We are owner-managed and rooted in Europe. With a strong focus on regulated and complex environments.

### Cross-disciplinary by design

Our teams bring together expertise in data, technology, process and people so you don’t get siloed advice.

### From strategy to everyday reality

We don’t stop at concepts. We support implementation, change and capability-building until results show up in daily work.

### Human, honest, hands-on

We work in close partnership with your teams, ask the uncomfortable questions and stay pragmatic and respectful.

## What we do:

Our work spans four fields that together create modern, resilient organisations.

### Data

We turn fragmented data into a reliable foundation for decisions – from data strategy and governance to analytics platforms.

### Technology

We design and implement architectures, platforms and cloud solutions that fit your business, your risk profile and your future.

### Process

We redesign and harmonise end-to-end processes so value flows – for customers, employees and partners.

### People

We support culture, leadership and skills so your people understand, support and drive the transformation in their daily work.

## The principles that guide our work.

We believe that transformation only works when it is human, connected and honest. We work together with our clients instead of at a distance. We are human-centered, because systems only change when people can and want to work with them.

We are pioneering, challenging old patterns and exploring new ways of solving problems. And we are result-driven and future-focused – measuring success by the performance and resilience your organisation gains.
