# We believe that transformation should create real, lasting performance. Not just new projects and presentations.

Our ambition is simple: To be the partner that makes complex transformation human, integrated and built for the long term.

What we believe in and how we work

## Our values guide how we work with clients, partners and each other.​

We connect data, technology, process and people so organisations can make better decisions, work more effectively and stay resilient in a changing world.

### Together

We work in true partnership – combining your expertise with our perspective across data, technology, process and people. We share responsibility for outcomes, not only for deliverables.

### Human-centered

Systems change only when people can and want to work with them. We design transformations that are understandable, transparent and respectful of the people who make them real.

### Pioneering

We are curious and willing to challenge established patterns. We explore new approaches and technologies where they add real value – not because they are fashionable.

### Result-driven

We focus on impact, not activity. We define clear goals, track progress and adapt when reality changes.

### Future-focused

We think beyond go-live dates and political cycles. We help build organisations, capabilities and architectures that stay relevant in the long term.

## Highlighted Solution Cases

Our services come to life in client work.

Here you’ll find a selection of solution cases that show how we connect data, technology, process and people to create real impact in different contexts and industries.

### Strengthening SMEs through a culture of continuous learning

Many SMEs (Small and Medium-sized Enterprises) want to develop their people but struggle to embed learning structurally in daily operations. Go for SLIM enables a practical, measurable approach that turns continuous learning into a natural part of everyday work.

### Data-driven labor market insights to address regional shortages

A medium-sized province uses ETIL’s ArbeidsmarktInZicht dashboard to replace outdated data with real-time vacancies, education, and trend insights to enable faster, targeted policy decisions in technology and healthcare

### Cutting workload and lead time with AI-driven email automation

An industrial manufacturer struggled with a flooded customer‑service mailbox, manual processing, and rising error rates. The AI Workflow Manager turns Outlook into an intelligent process hub that routes, enriches, and processes emails automatically.

### Optimization of unplanned maintenance at housing associations

24ROSA connects process logic, data insights, and partner collaboration to improve operational efficiency and deliver structural customer value.
