# Connected performance, shaped in the Netherlands.

ibc netherlands helps organisations move forward by improving how people, processes, leadership and performance work together. We combine consulting, experienced professionals and an integral approach to create progress that is measurable, human-centered and built to last.

## What defines ibc netherlands within the ibc group:

ibc netherlands stands for meaningful improvement in practice. As the Dutch organisation within the ibc group, founded in 1998, it combines business consulting, experienced interim support and a people-centered view on change to help organisations improve with clarity, energy and impact. With around 100 professionals and offices in Sittard, Maastricht and Utrecht, ibc netherlands brings together expertise, local presence and an integral approach to lasting organisational improvement.

### Improvement beyond advice

We help organisations understand where improvement is needed and how to move forward. Our work connects strategic ambition with practical action so that change becomes visible, manageable and sustainable.

### People, performance and progress

ibc netherlands looks at both the hard and soft side of organisations. We connect people, culture, processes, data and technology to improve performance in a way that works in real life.

### An integral view on change

Our approach is shaped by the ibc impact model. It helps us look at each challenge through the perspective of customers, business partners, employees and shareholders to build a fuller understanding of what drives meaningful improvement.

### A culture built on growth and collaboration

ibc netherlands combines professional ambition with a personal and down-to-earth culture. We value development, flexibility, honest collaboration and the shared drive to help organisations move forward.

## We believe progress only becomes meaningful when it works for both organisations and the people within them. That is why we combine insight, collaboration and practical action to create improvement that lasts. Not just in plans, but in daily reality.

## Built for lasting improvement.

ibc netherlands is strongest where organisations want to improve in a way that is both structured and human-centered. We support change initiatives that require clear analysis, strong stakeholder alignment and practical implementation — helping clients improve performance, strengthen organisations and build momentum for the long term.

### Business consulting

We design and support improvement and change programmes that help organisations move forward with focus and structure.

### Experient professionals

We provide experienced professionals who strengthen organisations in key interim and transformation roles.

### Impact-driven improvement

We use an integral view on performance to connect stakeholder needs, organisational goals and concrete actions.

### Continuous development

We help make progress visible and actionable so that improvement becomes part of the organisation, not just a one-time effort.
