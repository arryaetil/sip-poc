## How we work:

Senior advisory is about perspective and practicality at the same time. Board members and CIOs need a clear picture of where IT stands today – and what it should look like tomorrow.

Engagements start with listening: interviews, workshops and analyses of your IT landscape, projects and operating model. From there, scenarios and options are developed – always linked to business priorities, risks and financial implications.

Recommendations are concrete and implementable. If desired, our experts stay involved to accompany decisions, prepare major initiatives and support the handover from strategy to execution.

### Understand & assess

Gain a holistic view of IT strategy, organisation, projects and value streams. Including strengths, weaknesses and risks

### Design & decide

Develop strategic options, target pictures and roadmaps, and support leadership in making informed decisions

### Prepare & accompany execution

Translate decisions into programmes, initiatives and operating models. Including preparation of projects and support during the first phases of implementation

## Ask us anything

### When does senior advisory make sense?

Typically in phases of strategic reorientation, rapid growth, major transformations or when there is uncertainty about the status and direction of IT.

### Do you replace our CIO or IT leadership?

No. Senior advisory acts as a sparring partner and complement for existing leadership – providing external perspective, structure and capacity for strategic work.

### How long do typical senior advisory engagements last?

Depending on scope, from focused workshops and assessments over a few weeks to longer engagements that accompany strategy development and the preparation of major programmes.
