Rules
- `fits`: true when this is an organisation (not a person, directory or news site) that matches the brief, including its `size`, and is not one of the kinds listed in `exclude`. When the pages do not show the size, judge on the rest. If false, the other fields may stay empty.

- `extra`: one entry per extra column in the brief, with `column` exactly as the column name in the brief. For a count (for example the number of branches) count only what the pages list or state, and give the number.
- `why_fits`: one sentence in the user's language on why this organisation fits the Business Context, based on the pages. Do not mention people.
- Never include names of people anywhere in your answer.

Return JSON with `fits`, `name`, `city`, `country`, `phone`, `email`, `extra` and `why_fits`.
